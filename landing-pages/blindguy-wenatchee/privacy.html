Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Blind Guy of Wenatchee (“we,” “us,” or “our”), a part of The Blind Guy (blindguyoftricities.com), collects, uses, and protects the information you provide when you visit this landing page or request a free in-home estimate.

Information We Collect

We collect two kinds of information:

  • Information you give us. When you submit a form requesting an estimate, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and the product you’re interested in (blinds, shades, or shutters).
  • Information collected automatically. When you visit this page, we and our analytics/advertising partners may automatically collect your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring website, and marketing campaign identifiers (such as UTM parameters, Google Click ID “gclid,” and Facebook Click ID “fbclid”) through cookies and similar technologies.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your request and schedule or provide a free in-home estimate.
  • To contact you by phone, text, or email about your inquiry and our products and services.
  • To measure the performance of our advertising and improve our website and marketing.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud or misuse.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

This page uses Google Tag Manager to deploy analytics and advertising tools, which may include Google Analytics, Google Ads, and the Meta (Facebook) Pixel. These tools use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors interact with the page and to measure and optimize our advertising campaigns. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our business and website (for example, form, hosting, and email providers) and who are permitted to use it only to provide services to us.
  • Advertising and analytics partners such as Google and Meta, in connection with measuring and improving our advertising as described above.
  • Legal and safety purposes, when required by law or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of others.

Your Choices

  • You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by contacting us using the details below or following the unsubscribe instructions in our messages.
  • You can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings.
  • You can adjust Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com and opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s opt-out browser add-on.

Data Retention

We keep the information you provide for as long as needed to respond to your request, provide our services, and meet our legal and business obligations, after which we delete or de-identify it.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
